How we work

Aligned, client-side, and personally accountable.

The rules below are not marketing — they are the operating constraints of the firm.

Client-side only

We act for the owner, never the lender or the buyer. No commissions from banks, no placement fees, no product to push.

We never hold funds

Money moves directly between principals. Genio advises, structures and negotiates — it never holds or handles client funds.

Senior-led, every time

Every mandate is run personally by the principal, mobilizing the bench each deal needs — legal, modelling, sector operators.

Both sides of the corridor

Dubai-based, licensed UAE free zone company, with two decades of execution on both sides of the Saudi–UAE corridor.

Clients are never named — in our materials or our conversations. And clients introduced to Genio always remain free to choose their own advisors and providers.
