Refinancing a major mall through to completion

Real estate · Shareholder adviser and representative · Ongoing

Situation

A major Riyadh retail destination ran out of project funding before completion, and needed further finance to finish the mall and reach opening.

What was done

Restructured and extended over US$345m of bank financing to fund completion: a grace period, an added revolving line and a later increase on the same terms, with repayment tied to the mall's cash flow, and interest-rate hedging put in place.

Outcome

The mall was completed and opened, and is now leasing up with a clear path to higher operating income.
